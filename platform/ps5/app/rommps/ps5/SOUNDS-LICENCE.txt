RommPS's and RommDeck's interface sounds (assets/rommps/sounds in RommPS,

The cursor, select, tab, switch and setting sounds are mgs1-menu-move;
the rest come from the first set supplied.

Changes: converted from MP3 to 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV, one file per interface
cue (the same sound under several cue names where it serves several).
